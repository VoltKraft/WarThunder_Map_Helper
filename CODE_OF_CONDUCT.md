# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in this project regardless of background,
experience, identity, or personal circumstances.

Be respectful, give constructive technical feedback, and assume good intent
without excusing harmful behavior. Discuss ideas rather than attacking people.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of
another person's private information are not acceptable.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may remove
inappropriate content, restrict participation, or ban repeated or serious
violations. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so does not
expose private information or endanger participants.

Report concerns privately through the contact method on the
[maintainer's profile](https://github.com/VoltKraft). Maintainers will consider
reports confidentially and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters.
